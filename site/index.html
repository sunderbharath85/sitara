  • Enter recycles the rest
  • Always undoable
03
EditorPress E

Develop it.
Never damage it.

Edits are stored as data and rendered on the GPU. The original file is never written to, and Revert is one click — forever.

01Adjust

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation, clarity, dehaze, sharpening, vignette, grain and fade — plus one-click Auto.

02Looks & Color

14 built-in styles previewed on your own photo with a strength slider, and they sit under your sliders instead of replacing them. An 8-band hue/sat/lum mixer, black & white with its own mix, three-wheel grading, RGB and per-channel curves.

03Masks & Heal

Linear gradients, radial masks and a brush, each with its own adjustments. Click a blemish and a matching patch is found and colour-matched automatically; clone mode is there too.

04RAW

Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, DNG and more are developed in Rust straight from the sensor data, so highlights still have headroom to recover.

Before/after split, clipping warnings, a live histogram, unlimited undo, copy/paste settings between photos, and export to JPEG, PNG or WebP keeping camera details — and location only if you choose.

On-device AIStage 4 – 5 · shipped

Say it in words.
It stays on your machine.

Each model downloads once from its public source, is checked against a pinned SHA-256 digest, and then runs offline. Turn a feature off and its models and everything they learned are deleted.

ADescribe an edit

Restore an old photo

One click removes grain, brings blurred faces back, adds natural colour to black-and-white and sepia prints, and enlarges small scans.

Remove objects

Paint over a person, a sign or a speck and it is filled in from its surroundings.

Select subject, background, sky

Masks drawn by AI, each with its own full set of sliders.

Background blur

Depth-aware lens blur that keeps the subject sharp.

Denoise & Upscale

Real camera noise removed at full resolution. ×4 up to 1600 px, ×2 up to 3200 px, opened at 100% with before and after.

Search by meaning

Type “sunset over water” or “birthday cake” and find photos with no tags at all. CLIP, about 155 MB.

People

Faces found and grouped by person. Name someone once, merge look-alikes, drop the wrong matches. YuNet + SFace, about 39 MB.

Smart enhance

Automatic tone, plus brighter faces and a richer sky when those models are present.

GPU

It uses the graphics card you already paid for.

Any DirectX 12 GPU on Windows — NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Qualcomm — and Core ML on Apple silicon. Models a GPU cannot run stay on the processor, and one switch in Folders keeps all AI on the CPU.
